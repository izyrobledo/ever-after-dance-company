Meet Isabella

Isabella has had a lifelong career in dance. Starting in ballet at 3 years old, she developed a strong foundation in movement and a passion for dance. She danced with The Houston Ballet Academy for 8 years, performing with the Houston Ballet Company in The Nutcracker for many seasons. Through her childhood into adolescence, she perfected her carriage and technique before moving on to dance with Hope Stone Inc., a modern dance company based in Houston.

While studying Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin, she discovered swing dancing and fell in love with the art form. She has competed in Lindy Hop, Balboa, and Solo Jazz, winning awards across the country and internationally, including competitions in Houston, Los Angeles, Stockholm, and Vilnius, Lithuania.

Isabella has been teaching dance for 4 years, and loves seeing her students grow in confidence on and off the dance floor. As your wedding dance instructor, she brings a lifetime of knowledge and a love of dance that will leave you feeling energized and ready for your big day.
